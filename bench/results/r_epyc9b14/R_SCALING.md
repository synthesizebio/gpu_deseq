## Reading these numbers

- Small-*n* datasets may get **slower** with more workers: forking and
  serialising a chunked `DESeqDataSet` costs more than the per-gene fits it
  saves. That is a real result, not a misconfiguration.
- Parallel efficiency well under 100% at the best worker count is the point
  of this table: `DESeq2` leaves size-factor estimation, the dispersion trend
  fit, and parts of `results` serial, so the curve plateaus regardless of how
  many cores are available.
- Absolute serial times are only comparable across machines when CPU clock
  and BLAS match. Re-measure both the serial and parallel points on any new
  host rather than mixing a new parallel number against an old baseline.
